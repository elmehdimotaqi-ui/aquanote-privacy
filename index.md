# Aquanote — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 7 October 2026

Aquanote is an aquarium log for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to data when you use it.
It is the source of truth for the published page at
<https://elmehdimotaqi-ui.github.io/aquanote-privacy/>.

## What Aquanote stores

Your tanks (names, types, volumes and dimensions, start dates, units, tracked parameters and
target ranges, notes), water test results, water changes, dosing, feeding, maintenance and other
entries, livestock and plants, reminders, custom parameters and photos are stored **only on your
iPhone**, in the app's own storage. They are never uploaded, never synced, and never sent to us.
There is no account, no login, and no server belonging to Aquanote.

Your data is included in your iPhone backups (iCloud or computer), so a restore brings it back.
Deleting the app deletes all of it. **Delete All Data** in Settings removes every tank, test,
entry, livestock record, reminder, custom parameter and photo, cancels pending reminder
notifications and returns settings to their defaults, without deleting the app. It keeps only your
Pro purchase, your language choice and whether the app has already asked you for a rating.

Aquanote also stores small preferences on your device: your units, a default test kit, the tank
you last looked at, the language you picked, whether you have seen the note about target ranges,
how many tests you have saved and whether a rating was already requested (to decide when to ask
for an App Store rating, once).

## Camera and photos

Aquanote asks for camera access to photograph your tank and livestock, only when you first tap
Take Photo. Photos taken in the app and photos you pick from your library are resized and saved
as new JPEG files in the app's own storage, on the device, without the location and camera details
a photo can carry. Aquanote sees only the picture you choose, never the rest of your photo library.
It adds a picture to your library only when you choose **Save Image** from a photo's share sheet,
which is why iOS may ask you to allow adding photos.

## Notifications

Reminders (water changes, tests, filter cleaning and the like, or an alert when a test crosses a
limit you set) are **local notifications**, scheduled on your iPhone. Aquanote asks for permission
to send them when you save your first active reminder. Nothing is sent to or through a server.

## Export

With Aquanote Pro you can export your entries as a CSV file and a PDF tank report. The files are
created on your iPhone and go only where you send them from the share sheet. The copies the app
prepares are kept in its temporary storage, replaced at the next export and removed by Delete All
Data.

## What the ads collect

The free version of Aquanote shows ads through **Google AdMob**: a banner on the Timeline and
Insights screens, and occasionally a full-screen ad after you save an entry. AdMob is operated by
Google, and when it serves an ad it may collect:

- **Device identifiers**, including the advertising identifier if you allow tracking
- **Advertising data**, such as which ads were shown and interacted with
- **Product interaction data**, such as app opens and ad views
- **Approximate location** derived from the network connection, and **diagnostic data** (crash and
  performance information)

This data is collected by Google, not by us — we never receive it. Google's handling of it is
described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Your tanks, test results, notes, photos and their contents are never shared with AdMob or anyone
else.

## Tracking and your choice

When you first open Aquanote, before any ad is requested, it asks for permission to track your
activity across other companies' apps and websites. This is the standard iOS App Tracking
Transparency prompt.

- **If you allow it**, ads may be personalized.
- **If you decline**, or never answer, ads are requested as **non-personalized**. Aquanote works
  exactly the same either way — nothing about your tanks, tests, reminders or charts changes.

You can change your answer at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking** on your
iPhone, or from **Ad Tracking** in Aquanote's Settings.

## Aquanote Pro

**Aquanote Pro** is a one-time in-app purchase. When it is active, Aquanote shows no ads. The
purchase is handled by Apple; we never see your payment details. Your entitlement is checked with
Apple's StoreKit and is tied to your Apple Account, so you can restore it on your other devices.

## Children

Aquanote is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information
from them.
