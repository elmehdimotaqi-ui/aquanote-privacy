# Aquanote — Support

Aquanote is an aquarium log for iPhone: log your water tests in seconds, see each value against
your tank's targets, chart the trends and get reminded of water changes, entirely on your iPhone.

## Common questions

**How do I log a test?** On Home, tap **Log Test** (it is also in the + menu). Type the values from
your test kit; the last test's values show in grey as a guide, and each value is checked against
the tank's targets as you type. **Previous Field** and **Next Field** above the keyboard move
between rows. Tap **Save Test** when you are done; you only need one value to save.

**What does "Keep previous" do?** It carries the last value of that parameter into this test
without counting it as a new measurement; the row shows "Previous value kept". A field you leave
empty is carried over the same way, so you only type what you actually measured today.

**My test kit only measures a few parameters.** Tap **Test Kit** on the Log Test screen and pick
your kit: only the parameters it measures are shown. **All Parameters** shows everything the tank
tracks again. To start every test with your kit, choose it in Settings → Log test → Default test
kit. Which parameters a tank tracks, and in which order, is set in the tank's settings under
**Parameters & Targets**.

**Are the target ranges right for my tank?** The default ranges are general hobby guidance, drawn
from test-kit makers' instructions and widely used reef and freshwater references, for the type of
tank you chose. Adjust them for your livestock
in the tank's **Parameters & Targets**: set a minimum, a maximum or both, and an amber margin
(values that close to a limit show amber). **Reset to Default** brings back the original range.

**Can I use gallons, Fahrenheit or specific gravity?** Yes. Settings → Units sets volume (liters,
US or UK gallons), temperature (°C or °F), salinity (ppt or specific gravity), hardness (dGH / dKH,
ppm or meq/L) and length (cm or inches). A tank can have its own units in its settings
(**Custom units**). Changing units never changes what you logged, only how it is shown.

**Reminders don't arrive.** Check that notifications are allowed for Aquanote in the iPhone's
Settings → Notifications; if they are off, the Reminders screen says so and offers **Turn On in
Settings**. Paused reminders and the reminders of an archived tank never notify. A reminder "When
a Test Crosses a Limit" is checked each time you save a test, using measured values only, and
alerts you once until the value is back.

**What does the free version include?** One tank, two active reminders and the last 60 days of
history (older entries are kept and come back with Pro), with ads. Aquanote Pro, a one-time
purchase, adds more tanks, your full history, unlimited reminders, custom parameters, chart
comparison and CSV and PDF export, and removes the ads.

**I bought Pro on another iPhone.** Open Settings in the app and tap **Restore Purchases** under
Aquanote Pro. The purchase is tied to your Apple Account.

**How do I export my log?** With Pro, Settings → Export prepares a spreadsheet (CSV) and a PDF
tank report for one tank or all of them, archived tanks included; share them to Files, Mail or any
other app from the share sheet.

**Where is my data stored?** On your iPhone only, and in its backups. Nothing is uploaded. There is
no account to create and no password to lose.

**How do I move to a new iPhone?** Aquanote has no account and no sync, so your log moves with
your iPhone backup: set up the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one (or with
Quick Start), and your tanks, tests and photos come with it. Deleting the app deletes its data, so
keep a backup before you do.

**Which languages?** English, French and Arabic. Change the app's language in Settings → Language;
it applies the next time Aquanote opens.

**Why does the app ask to track?** The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. The prompt lets
you choose personalized or non-personalized ads; the app works the same either way, and Pro shows
no ads at all.

**How do I delete everything?** Settings → Delete All Data removes every tank, test, entry,
livestock record, reminder, custom parameter and photo, and returns settings to their defaults;
your Pro purchase is kept. To remove a single tank, use **Delete Tank** in its settings. Deleting
the app also deletes all of its data.
